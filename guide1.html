Board game night · Rules rundown

Dead of
Winter

A semi-cooperative zombie survival game for tonight's crew of five. You're a colony of survivors trying to make it through a brutal winter — together. Probably together.

Everyone works toward one shared goal, but each player also has a secret personal objective — and one of you might secretly be a betrayer trying to sink the whole colony.

How you win (or don't)

Three ways the game ends

The colony wins

Complete the main objective (drawn at the start — e.g. collect enough samples, stockpile food) before the round tracker runs out AND complete your own secret objective. Yes, you need both to personally win.

The colony loses

If morale hits zero or the round tracker runs out before the main objective is done, everyone loses — except a betrayer, who might be quietly celebrating.

The betrayer wins

If someone drew the betrayer secret objective, they win by getting the colony to fail while meeting their own conditions. There isn't always a betrayer — that's the fun part.

Exile

Suspect someone? The group can vote to exile a player. They're not out of the game — they get a new exile objective and keep playing from outside the colony.

A round, step by step

What actually happens on your turn

  1. A crisis is revealed

    Each round, the colony faces a crisis (e.g. contribute 4 food or bad things happen). Players contribute cards face down — so a betrayer can secretly sabotage it with wrong cards.

  2. Roll your action dice

    You roll a pool of dice at the start of your turn, then spend them on actions: attack zombies, search locations for cards, build barricades, or clean waste. Higher rolls unlock harder actions.

  3. Move & take actions

    Your survivors can stay at the colony or travel to locations (hospital, grocery store, police station...) to search for the stuff you need. Moving and fighting risks the exposure die — see below.

  4. Zombies swarm

    At the end of the round, zombies spawn at the colony and locations based on how many survivors (and how much noise) are there. If there's no space left, they start killing people.

  5. Feed the colony & check morale

    The colony consumes food every round. Starvation, deaths, and piled-up waste all drop morale. Morale at zero = game over.

The exposure die ☠

Every time you move between locations or attack a zombie, you roll this little die of doom:

·Nothing (most faces)
⚔Wound
❄Frostbite — keeps wounding
🦷Bite — instant death, and it can spread

The bite is what makes everyone at the table go quiet. Roll it while your survivor is standing next to others and the infection can chain.

Crossroads cards

The signature twist

On your turn, the player to your right holds a secret Crossroads card. It has a hidden trigger — something like "if this player moves to the hospital..." If you happen to do that thing, the card fires and a mini story event happens, usually with a tough choice attached.

You never know what will set one off, which makes every turn feel like a little episode of a zombie TV show. Just read them dramatically. It's the law.

Tips for your first game

Five things worth knowing going in

Talk, but don't overshare. Cooperation wins the game, but everyone has a secret objective — a little paranoia is healthy and hilarious.

Don't hoard survivors in one spot. More people at a location = more zombies spawn there.

Feed the colony first. Food shortages quietly drain morale faster than zombies do.

Watch who fails crisis checks. If a crisis fails when the contributed cards should've covered it, someone slipped in a sabotage card...

Don't exile-vote too fast. A wrong exile costs morale and a friend. Sometimes bad luck is just bad luck.
